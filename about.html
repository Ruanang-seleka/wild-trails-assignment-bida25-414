About Us

WildTrails Botswana

Who we are

WildTrails Botswana is a locally owned and operated safari company dedicated to showcasing the natural wonders of Botswana to visitors from around the world. Founded by passionate Batswana conservationists, we believe that responsible tourism can transform lives, protect wildlife, and preserve the landscapes we all love.

With over 10 years of experience guiding travellers through the Okavango Delta, Chobe, the Kalahari and beyond, our team brings deep local knowledge, genuine passion, and an unwavering commitment to delivering extraordinary safari experiences.

Our Purpose

WildTrails Botswana Mission

Our mission is to connect travellers with the raw, untouched beauty of Botswana through responsible, sustainable, and community-based safari experiences. We aim to be Botswana's most trusted safari partner by delivering exceptional service, preserving wildlife habitats, and uplifting the communities we operate in.

Every safari we run contributes a portion of its revenue directly to local conservation projects and community development initiatives across Botswana.

Looking Ahead

WildTrails Vision

Our vision is a Botswana where wildlife thrives, communities prosper, and every visitor leaves with a deep and lasting connection to the African wilderness. We envision a future where responsible tourism is the standard, not the exception.

By 2030 we aim to expand our operations to cover all major wildlife corridors in Botswana, partner with 20 local communities, and achieve carbon-neutral safari operations across our entire fleet.

What We Stand For

WildTrails Botswana Values

Conservation

We are committed to protecting Botswana's wildlife and natural environments. A portion of every safari booking goes directly towards conservation projects and anti-poaching initiatives.

Community

We believe tourism should benefit local communities. We employ local guides, purchase local produce, and actively invest in community education and development programmes.

Sustainability

Our operations are designed to have the lowest possible environmental impact. We use eco-friendly camps, minimise waste, and are working towards fully carbon-neutral operations.

Exceptional Service

Every guest deserves an extraordinary experience. Our guides are trained to the highest standards, and our team is available 24/7 before, during, and after your safari.
