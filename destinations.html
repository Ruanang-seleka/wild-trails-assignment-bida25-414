Our Destinations

Discover four of Botswana's most spectacular wildlife destinations, each offering a unique and unforgettable safari experience.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Okavango Delta

The Okavango Delta is the world's largest inland delta and one of Africa's most breathtaking wildlife destinations. Fed by seasonal floodwaters from Angola, this unique ecosystem supports an extraordinary diversity of wildlife.

Activities include mokoro canoe trips, walking safaris, game drives, and bird watching. Best visited between June and October when water levels are highest.

Africa's Elephant Capital

Chobe National Park

Chobe National Park is home to the world's largest concentration of elephants, with over 50,000 roaming its plains. The Chobe River offers spectacular sunset boat cruises where you can watch elephants swim and hippos laze in the water.

Game drives here offer incredible sightings of lions, leopards, buffalo, and countless bird species. The park is accessible year-round with different highlights each season.

Ancient Wilderness

Kalahari Desert

The Kalahari is not a true desert but a vast semi-arid sandy savanna spanning Botswana, Namibia, and South Africa. This ancient landscape is home to the San Bushmen, the oldest indigenous people on earth.

Expect to see meerkats, brown hyenas, black-maned Kalahari lions, and stunning star-filled night skies. Best visited between March and May after the summer rains when the vegetation is lush and green.

Salt Flats Wonder

Makgadikgadi Pans

The Makgadikgadi Pans are one of the largest salt flats in the world, covering over 12,000 square kilometres. During the dry season the pans are a vast shimmering white expanse. After the rains they transform into a lush green paradise.

The pans host one of Africa's largest zebra and wildebeest migrations, as well as huge flocks of flamingos. Quad biking across the salt flats under the stars is an unforgettable experience.
